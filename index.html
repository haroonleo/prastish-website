Hazrat Dawood ke Zaboor

Ancient worship, presented with modern musical excellence.

Prastish exists to preserve the depth of Zaboor worship and present it in a way that feels fresh, beautiful and accessible for young people, families and churches in the UK and beyond.

Through instrumental releases, live sessions and future worship resources, the vision is to help a new generation rediscover prayer, scripture and worship through music.
