About Prastish

Worship. Music. Hope. Community.

Prastish is a Christian worship and creative arts initiative founded by Haroon Leo to inspire faith, preserve worship heritage and serve communities through music, media and live worship experiences.

Through instrumental worship, Zaboor resources and creative collaborations, Prastish exists to help people encounter hope and worship with fresh devotion.

✝

Our heart

To create worship moments that are beautiful, prayerful and accessible for churches, families and the next generation.
