About Prastish

Worship. From the heart. For His glory.

Prastish means Worship. It is a Christian worship music project founded by Haroon Leo to create music that helps people encounter peace, hope, faith and the presence of God.

Rooted in South Asian Christian heritage, Prastish brings together Psalms, worship, rubab, strings, guitars and modern studio production to create a sound that is both ancient and new.
