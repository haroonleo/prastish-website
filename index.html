Who We Are

Prastish means Worship.

Prastish is an independent Christian arts initiative founded by Haroon Leo to create original South Asian Christian worship music and sacred arts for communities across the UK and beyond.

Rooted in faith and shaped by South Asian musical heritage, Prastish brings together worship, composition, production, education and collaboration to inspire peace, hope, faith and spiritual encouragement.

The Organisation

Four pillars of Prastish

Prastish Worship

Worship nights, psalm projects, church events, gospel albums and Christian music gatherings.

Prastish Arts

Concerts, festivals, commissions, cross-cultural collaborations and public performances.

Prastish Academy

Workshops, mentoring, music education, youth programmes and training for worship musicians.

Prastish Studios

Recording, production, publishing, film, digital content, live sessions and creative media.

Vision

A global platform for South Asian Christian music.

The long-term vision is to develop Prastish into a global Christian arts organisation producing worship albums, tours, workshops, youth conferences, artist collaborations and educational resources.

Prastish will create space for worshippers, musicians and young people to grow in faith, creativity and excellence while sharing the hope of Christ through music.
