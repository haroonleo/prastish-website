About Prastish

A calling to inspire faith through worship and creative arts.

Prastish exists to create spaces where people can encounter God through worship, music, storytelling, and community. Founded by Haroon Leo, the vision is rooted in Christian faith and shaped by years of music, ministry, production and live worship experience.

Prastish is being developed as a UK-based Christian creative initiative with a long-term vision to serve churches, families, young people and communities through worship resources, live gatherings, media, education and outreach.

“Our heart is to use every song, every note and every creative gift to point people towards hope in Christ.”

Haroon LeoFounder, Prastish

Vision & Mission

To inspire hope, deepen faith and strengthen communities through worship, music and creative arts.

Prastish is building a platform for worship, heritage, creativity and community engagement, with a special heart for young people, churches and the South Asian Christian diaspora in the UK.

Hazrat Dawood ke Zaboor

Preserving worship heritage for the next generation.

One of Prastish's core visions is to preserve and share Hazrat Dawood ke Zaboor, helping young Christians and families in the UK reconnect with the beauty of these worship traditions, especially those growing up away from Punjabi and Urdu language contexts.

Through music, translation, storytelling and digital resources, Prastish aims to make this worship heritage accessible, meaningful and spiritually powerful for today's generation.
