About Prastish

A worship movement rooted in faith, music and service.

Prastish was founded by Haroon Leo as a dedicated Christian worship and creative arts initiative. The vision is to use music, live worship, storytelling and digital media to encourage faith, serve churches and reach families, young people and communities.

Through worship projects, recordings, live gatherings and creative partnerships, Prastish aims to build a platform where people can experience hope, encounter God and reconnect with the richness of Christian worship heritage.

Founded by Haroon Leo

Hazrat Dawood ke Zaboor

Preserving worship heritage for the next generation.

One of the core Prastish visions is to preserve and share Hazrat Dawood ke Zaboor, helping young Christians and families in the UK reconnect with the beauty of these worship traditions, especially those growing up away from Punjabi and Urdu language contexts.

Through music, translation, storytelling and digital resources, Prastish aims to make this worship heritage accessible, meaningful and spiritually powerful for today's generation.
